This Rust crate implements a small order-pricing pipeline split across ten modules. Several steps are
unfinished (`todo!()`): validation, per-line pricing, coupon value, cart subtotal — and the money
rounding in the tax/coupon steps does not follow the project's rounding policy yet.

Make the crate's test suite pass (`cargo test`). Do not modify the tests.

The project documents its rounding policy under `docs/`; follow it exactly, including in the new code
you write. Keep each concern in the module that owns it instead of inlining everything into `lib.rs`.
